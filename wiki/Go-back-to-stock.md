The Turtle Beach firmware is not included here, but you can put it back at any time. Like every update, this **erases your calibration and settings**.

## With VelocityOne Tuner (Windows)

Update the shifter in VelocityOne Tuner as usual. Tuner installs its own firmware over OpenShift.

## With the web tool

You need the stock firmware file, `VelocityOneTuner_Shift_Firmware.bin`, which comes with VelocityOne Tuner.

1. Connect the shifter in the **[web tool](https://filipnone.github.io/OpenShifter/)** and open the **Firmware** tab.
2. Choose **A file on this computer** and pick the `.bin` file.
3. Press **Install stock firmware**, then **Erase and install**.
4. If the page asks, press **Allow update mode**, pick **VelocityOne Multi-Shift** and press **Connect**.
5. Keep the shifter plugged in until the page says the stock firmware is installed.

## Afterwards

Calibrate in VelocityOne Tuner, or in the web tool's **Calibration** tab, which works with stock firmware too.
